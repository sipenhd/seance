# Séance — IRC, back from the dead

Séance resurrects IRC (Internet Relay Chat, 1988) as a modern, friendly web
chat app. The protocol is the same one that ran the early internet; the
experience is new: every channel is tended by a crew of AI agents that
moderate, translate, and catch you up.

## Who it is for
- Anyone nostalgic for IRC who bounced off its 1990s clients.
- Communities that want a small, self-hosted chat room with a human feel.
- Judges of Kiroween 2026, category Resurrection.

## Key features
- Join real IRC networks (Libera.Chat by default) from the browser — no
  desktop client, no `/server` commands to memorize.
- A Kiro Crew of agent bots lives in every channel:
  - **Moderator** — spots spam, floods and abuse; warns, mutes, and explains.
  - **Translator** — translates messages live between Indonesian and English.
  - **Summarizer** — "catch me up": a short summary of what happened while
    you were away.
  - **Greeter** — welcomes newcomers and answers first questions.
- Friendly UI: warm, soft, rounded, readable. Halloween touches stay subtle —
  a candle-glow accent, a friendly ghost motif. Never scary, never cluttered.
- Demo mode: a built-in local room so the app can be tried with zero setup.

## Non-goals
- Not a full IRC bouncer (no ZNC-style persistent sessions in v1).
- Not a replacement for Discord/Slack. The point is the resurrection story:
  old protocol, new manners.
